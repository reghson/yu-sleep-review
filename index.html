What is it?

So, what is Yu Sleep?

According to the official site, Yu Sleep is an all-natural, plant-based liquid sleep formula that you take as drops before bed. The vendor describes it as "nano-enhanced" and says it's designed to help you fall asleep faster, stay asleep through the night and wake up refreshed, while working with your body's natural rhythm instead of relying on high-dose melatonin.

How it works

How the official site says to use it

Keep it by your bed

The vendor suggests keeping the bottle on your bedside table so you don't forget it.

Two droppers, 30 minutes before bed

Squeeze two full droppers into your mouth (under the tongue is best, they say), hold for 30 seconds, then swallow.

Stay consistent

The vendor recommends at least 21 nights in a row, and says the fuller "sleep system reset" it describes takes 3 to 6 months.

The official site says it supports your brain's "sleep pressure" system (adenosine), natural melatonin release and a calmer nervous system. These are the vendor's claims, and results vary from person to person.

Is it a fit?

Who it's for, and who should skip it

It may suit you if…

  • You're a healthy adult who has the odd restless night
  • You want a lower-dose melatonin option
  • You're happy to take something nightly for at least a few weeks
  • You're also working on your sleep habits

Skip it, or ask your doctor first, if…

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Yu Sleep a cure for insomnia?

No. It's a dietary supplement, not a medicine, and it isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. If you have trouble sleeping most nights, talk to a doctor.

How fast does it work?

The official site says most people notice better sleep within 3–7 days, and its refund page says the real change usually comes between weeks two and four. Everyone is different, and results vary.

Will it make me groggy in the morning?

The vendor positions it as a gentler option than high-dose melatonin, with 0.9 mg per serving. How you feel will depend on you, so don't drive or operate machinery if you feel drowsy.

Can I buy just one bottle?

No. The official site only sells 2, 3 and 6-month packages. The vendor says it chose this because it believes the full effect takes months of consistent use.

Does it ship outside the US?

Yes, according to the official FAQ. It says orders ship within 24 hours, arriving in 5–7 business days in the US and 15–21 business days elsewhere.
